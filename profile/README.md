# MyPageAudit

Evidence-based website audits and monitoring that help developers and small teams find, prioritize and verify SEO improvements.

Our goal is to turn website diagnostics into a repeatable improvement cycle: **inspect → understand → prioritize → fix → verify**. Findings should show their evidence, explain their impact and acknowledge what a page audit cannot measure.

MyPageAudit crawls discoverable same-origin HTML pages and sitemaps, analyzes each page independently and shows evidence, image counts and coverage limits. We maintain an accessible interface, a reliable API, reusable diagnostic libraries and portable infrastructure with private operations dashboards and recoverable backups. Recurring monitoring, agency workflows and integrations are roadmap items.

The organization repository owns the roadmap, cross-repository issues and release notes. Component repositories own implementation and tests.
