# MyPageAudit

Website audits with clear evidence and practical next steps. Inspect public HTML, understand a finding, fix it and compare a later audit.

## Current Free beta

- Single-page and bounded site crawling with 32 checks for page metadata, technical SEO, mobile declarations, headings, images and schema.
- Evidence, recommendations, one saved project, owned history and technical competitor comparisons.
- Branded PDF export and requested PDF report emails with saved permission and quota.
- Google/verified-email accounts, appearance, support, reviews and privacy controls.
- Optional free Ahrefs Domain Rating when configured; missing measurements stay unavailable.

Free is the only current offer. AI insights and backlink requests are disabled. Keywords and Rank Tracker are Upcoming. Automatic daily/monthly reports are paused. Crawls inspect source HTML, respect robots and disclose coverage limits; scores are not search-ranking guarantees.

Report a reproducible bug in its component repo; propose product work in product and cross-repo engineering work in organization. Follow the [contribution guide](https://github.com/mypageaudit/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md). For sensitive reports use [private security reporting](https://github.com/mypageaudit/.github/blob/main/SECURITY.md), not public issues.
